Welcome to AI Tech University International Community

Welcome International Students

At AI Tech University, we proudly welcome students from around the world to join our diverse and inclusive academic community. We believe that cultural diversity enriches the learning experience and encourages the exchange of ideas, perspectives, and innovation. Our dedicated International Student Office is committed to supporting students throughout every stage of their academic journey—from the application process and visa guidance to campus life and graduation.
Whether you are pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree, AI Tech University provides a welcoming environment, world-class education, modern facilities, and comprehensive student support services to help you achieve your academic and professional goals.

Admission Requirement

International applicants who wish to study at AI Tech University must meet the University's academic and English language requirements before admission can be offered. Applicants are encouraged to prepare all required documents carefully to ensure a smooth and timely application process.

    To apply, international students must submit the following documents:
  • A completed online application form.
  • Official academic transcripts and certificates from previous educational institutions.
  • A valid passport with at least six months of remaining validity.
  • Proof of English language proficiency (IELTS, TOEFL, Duolingo English Test, or an equivalent approved examination).
  • A personal statement explaining academic goals and career aspirations.
  • One or two letters of recommendation (if required by the selected program).
  • A current résumé or curriculum vitae (required for selected graduate programs).
  • Financial documents demonstrating the ability to cover tuition fees and living expenses.
  • Any additional program-specific documents requested by the Admissions Office.
All documents submitted in a language other than English must be accompanied by an official English translation. Applications will be reviewed by the Admissions Committee, and successful applicants will receive an official Offer of Admission with instructions for enrollment and student visa processing.

English Language Requirements

Since English is the primary language of instruction at AI Tech University, all international applicants whose first language is not English must demonstrate English language proficiency through one of the following approved examinations.

Minimum English Language Requirements

IELTS Academic 6.5 Overall

Note: Some academic programs may require higher English language scores. Applicants are encouraged to review the specific admission requirements for their chosen program before applying.

Scholarship for International Students

AI Tech University is committed to attracting talented students from around the world by offering a variety of scholarships to support their academic journey. Our international scholarships are awarded based on academic excellence, leadership potential, extracurricular achievements, and outstanding personal accomplishments. These scholarships are designed to reduce the financial burden of studying abroad and encourage students to pursue their educational goals in a diverse and innovative learning environment.

Global Excellence Scholarship

This prestigious scholarship is awarded to exceptional international students with outstanding academic achievements and leadership qualities.

  • 100% Tuition Scholarship
  • 75% Tuition Scholarship
  • 50% Tuition Scholarship

International Merit Scholarship

Designed for academically strong students who have consistently demonstrated excellent performance throughout their previous education.

  • 40% Tuition Scholarship
  • 30% Tuition Scholarship
  • 20% Tuition Scholarship

Future Innovators Scholarship

Awarded to students who have shown exceptional talent in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, Data Science, Software Engineering, robotics, programming competitions, or scientific research.

Awards ranging from 30% to 70% of annual tuition fees.

Global Leaders Scholarship

This scholarship recognizes students who have demonstrated outstanding leadership, community service, volunteer work, entrepreneurship, or significant contributions to their schools and communities.

Awards ranging from 25% to 60% of annual tuition fees.

Research Excellence Scholarship (Master's Students)

Available exclusively to international students enrolling in Master's degree programs. This scholarship supports students with excellent academic records and a strong interest in research and innovation.

Awards ranging from 50% to 100% of annual tuition fees.

Scholarship Renewal

Most scholarships are renewable each academic year, provided students maintain the required Grade Point Average (GPA), demonstrate satisfactory academic progress, and comply with the University's scholarship policies.

Important Note: Receiving a scholarship does not automatically include accommodation, health insurance, travel expenses, visa application fees, or other personal living costs unless specifically stated in the scholarship offer. Students are encouraged to review the terms and conditions of each scholarship before submitting their application.

Accommodation

AI Tech University offers safe, comfortable, and affordable accommodation options to ensure that international students feel at home throughout their academic journey. Whether students choose to live on campus or in nearby private residences, the University is committed to providing a supportive living environment that promotes academic success, personal well-being, and cultural exchange.

On-Campus Accommodation

Our on-campus residence halls are designed to provide a secure and welcoming environment for students from around the world. Rooms are fully furnished and include essential amenities such as high-speed Wi-Fi, study desks, comfortable beds, wardrobes, laundry facilities, and 24-hour security. Living on campus also gives students convenient access to classrooms, libraries, laboratories, dining facilities, and recreational centers.

Off-campus Accommodation

Students who prefer greater independence may choose from a variety of private apartments, shared accommodations, and student residences located near the University. The International Student Office provides guidance and resources to help students find safe, affordable, and reliable housing options close to campus.

Accommodation facilities

Students living in University accommodation have access to a wide range of facilities, including:
  • Fully furnished single and shared rooms.
  • High-speed internet access throughout the residence halls.
  • Study lounges and collaborative learning spaces.
  • Common kitchens and dining areas.
  • Laundry facilities.
  • 24-hour security and CCTV surveillance.
  • Fitness and recreational areas.
  • Student support and residential staff available to assist when needed.

Accommodation Fee

Accommodation fees vary depending on the type of residence and room selected. Detailed information regarding housing options, room availability, and current accommodation charges will be provided after a student receives an official offer of admission.
Important Note:Since on-campus housing is limited, international students are strongly encouraged to apply for accommodation as early as possible. Rooms are allocated on a first-come, first-served basis after admission has been confirmed
