Welcome to AI Tech University Admission Page

At AI Tech University, we are committed to providing a seamless and efficient admission process for prospective students. Our goal is to ensure that every applicant has the opportunity to join our vibrant academic community and pursue their passion for technology and innovation. At AI Tech University, we believe every student deserves the opportunity to build a successful future in technology. Our admission process is designed to be simple, transparent, and accessible for students from around the world. Whether you are applying for an undergraduate or postgraduate program, our admissions team is committed to helping you through every step of your application journey. We welcome ambitious students who are passionate about innovation, problem-solving, and creating the technologies of tomorrow. If you are ready to begin your journey in Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science, Cybersecurity, Data Science, or Software Engineering, we encourage you to apply today.

Admission Requirements

  • Completed application form
  • Official transcripts from previous educational institutions
  • Letters of recommendation (if applicable)
  • Statement of purpose or personal essay
  • Standardized test scores (if required)
  • Proof of English proficiency (for international students)

Note: For International Students, the requirement is to submit a valid English proficiency test score. For more information, please click here.

Application Process

  1. Complete the online application form available on our website.
  2. Submit all required documents, including transcripts and letters of recommendation.
  3. Pay the application fee (if applicable).
  4. Attend an interview (if required).
  5. Receive an admission decision via email or through your applicant portal.

Admission Intakes

AI Tech University offer two main admission intakes each year:

  • Fall Intake: Typically begins in August
  • Spring Intake: Typically begins in January

Contact Our Admissions Team

If you have any questions or need assistance with the admission process, please do not hesitate to contact our admissions team. We are here to help you every step of the way.
