Welcome to AI Tech University Tuition Fee Page

At AI Tech University, we are committed to providing a world-class education at a competitive and affordable cost. Our tuition fees are designed to reflect the quality of education, modern facilities, experienced faculty, and outstanding student support services we provide. Tuition fees may vary depending on the academic program and level of study. Students are encouraged to review the fee structure carefully and contact the Admissions Office if they require further information regarding scholarships, financial aid, or payment plans.

Scholarships and Financial Aid

At AI Tech University, we believe that financial circumstances should never prevent talented students from achieving their academic goals. To recognize academic excellence and support students with financial needs, the University offers a range of scholarships and financial aid programs. Scholarships are awarded based on academic performance, leadership qualities, extracurricular achievements, community service, and financial need. Students are encouraged to apply early, as scholarship opportunities are limited and awarded on a competitive basis.

Merit Excellence Scholarship

Awarded to students with outstanding academic records and exceptional achievements.

  • 100% Tuition Scholarship – For students with exceptional academic performance and leadership.
  • 75% Tuition Scholarship – For students with excellent academic results and strong extracurricular involvement.
  • 50% Tuition Scholarship – For students who demonstrate consistent academic excellence.

Academic Achievement Scholarship

Designed for students who have maintained high grades throughout their previous education.

  • 40% Tuition Scholarship
  • 30% Tuition Scholarship
  • 20% Tuition Scholarship

Financial Assistance Scholarship

Available for students who demonstrate financial need while maintaining satisfactory academic performance.

Up to 60% Tuition Assistance. Renewable each academic year, subject to eligibility requirements.

Leadership & Community Service Scholarship

Awarded to students who have shown outstanding leadership, volunteer work, or significant contributions to their communities.

Scholarship value ranging from 25% to 50% of annual tuition fees.

Innovation & Technology Scholarship

This scholarship is offered to students who have demonstrated exceptional talent in programming, robotics, artificial intelligence, software development, research, or technology competitions.

Awards ranging from 30% to 70% of annual tuition fees.

All scholarship applications are carefully reviewed by the University's Scholarship Committee. Recipients are expected to maintain satisfactory academic progress and comply with the University's scholarship renewal policies.

Important Note: Scholarships listed on this page are available for domestic students. If you are an international student, please click here to check your scholarship eligibility, available funding opportunities, application requirements, and application deadlines.
